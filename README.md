Smart Document Format (SDF)

An Open Concept for Smarter, Context-Aware Documents

Smart Document Format (SDF) is an early-stage concept proposed by Tannu Kumari.

The idea explores a smarter way of interacting with digital documents, where readers can access contextual explanations of words, terms, or concepts while reading.

• The Vision

Traditional documents mainly display information. SDF explores how documents could make information easier to understand by providing relevant explanations within the reading experience.

The long-term vision is to explore a document format that could support smarter, more accessible, and context-aware reading.

• Project Goals

- Explore the concept of intelligent, context-aware documents.
- Make complex information easier to understand.
- Encourage innovation in digital document experiences.
- Create opportunities for developers and researchers to contribute.

• Current Status

SDF is an early-stage concept under development.

This repository serves as a public record of the concept and its ongoing development. It does not yet represent a finalized file-format standard or a complete software implementation.

• Open for Collaboration

Developers, researchers, students, and innovators are welcome to explore this concept, suggest improvements, and develop independent implementations.

The project is open to discussion, collaboration, and future development.
